About CoreLabTech

HBOT Monitoring & Research

CoreLabTech is a research platform for structured observation of physiological response before, during and after hyperbaric oxygen therapy sessions.

Research Platform Technology
Session phases Check-in / Session / Recovery
Wearable input HR & HRV Timeline
Oximeter input SpO2 & Pulse Timeline
Analysis output Wellness response + data quality

What We Monitor

The platform focuses on research-grade signals that help evaluate oxygenation, cardiovascular response and recovery patterns.

SpO2

Blood oxygen saturation before, during and after chamber exposure.

Pulse & Heart Rate

Pulse from the SpO2/pulse timeline and heart rate from the HR/HRV timeline.

HRV

Heart rate variability from RR intervals for autonomic response review.

Controlled research workflow

From chamber telemetry to session report

CoreLabTech combines chamber context, wearable data and pulse oximeter measurements into one synchronized session record. This makes it easier to review sensor quality, compare check-in and recovery response, and inspect the synchronized session timeline.

HBOT Session Flow

Each stage preserves context for later analysis and report generation.

Check-in Baseline Check

Record SpO2 and pulse before entering the chamber.

Session Chamber Monitoring

Collect HR/HRV and SpO2/pulse timelines during the session.

Recovery Recovery Check

Compare post-session values with baseline response.

AI Wellness Analysis

Generate score, session flags, findings and wellness report output.

Why It Matters

The goal is not diagnosis. The goal is clean, inspectable data that supports repeatable research review.

Objective Data

Structured signals reduce reliance on subjective notes after a session.

Recovery Insight

Check-in to recovery comparison helps show how oxygenation and pulse changed.

Safety Review Layer

Low SpO2, elevated pulse/HR or low HRV are highlighted for review.

Important note

CoreLabTech is a wellness monitoring platform. AI scores and session flags are not a medical diagnosis and should not replace clinical evaluation.
